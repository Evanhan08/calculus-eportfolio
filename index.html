About Me

My name is Stephen John Cortez, I am a second-year student taking Bachelor of Science in Information Technology – Web Development. I am interested in designing digitally, programming, and creating different digital projects.

To be honest, I was expecting 2CALC-IT to be really tough. Calculus has a reputation for being super overwhelming with all the math and problem-solving, so I was pretty nervous at first. But as the semester went on, I actually started enjoying it way more than I thought I would. It didn't feel as stressful as I expected, and I really liked figuring out how all the different concepts fit together.

One of the best parts of the course was getting to help out my classmates. I even made some quick tutorial videos where I broke down the topics in my own words. Instead of just repeating what the professor said in class, I tried to explain the lessons in a simpler way that my classmates could follow more easily.

Through this journey, 2CALC-IT taught me that you don't fully get a topic until you can teach it to someone else. Calculus became not only a subject where I solved mathematical problems but also an opportunity to share what I learned.

My Calculus Learning Journey

Here's an overview of the topics we studied throughout the course and the activities that show my work for each topic. These artifacts include worksheets and examinations that document my learning and understanding of Calculus.

  1. Composition of Functions – Understanding how one function can be combined with another by using function composition.
  2. Evaluating Limits – Learning how to evaluate limits and determine the value a function approaches as its input approaches a particular value.
  3. Basic Differentiation Rules – Learning the basic rules used to find the derivatives of mathematical functions.
  4. Product Rule, Quotient Rule & Fractional Exponents – Applying differentiation rules to functions involving products, quotients, and fractional exponents.
  5. Chain Rule – Using the chain rule to differentiate composite functions.
  6. Prelim Examination – A collection of examination work covering the topics included in the prelim period.
  7. Midterm Examination – A collection of examination work covering the topics included in the midterm period.
  8. Group 3 Projects – Three group projects: a Calculus infographic, a card deck on limits, and a poster on function operations.

My Growth in Calculus

Going into 2CALC-IT, I knew Calculus would take some effort, but since I've been pretty comfortable with solving math since SHS, I wasn't super worried. What really surprised me, though, was how much I actually enjoyed the subject as we went along.
